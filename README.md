# ICM_Finance_Classwork

## The 12-exercise list

| No. | Exercise | What participants will do | Final output |
|---:|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Bank statement versus company bank-book reconciliation** | Match transactions, investigate repeated entries and explain differences between two records | Reconciliation statement and exception report |
| **2** | **Financing dues, collections and overdue ageing** | Calculate unpaid scheduled dues, distinguish overdue/current/future amounts and summarise borrower balances | Receivables tracker, ageing dashboard and internal review note |
| **3** | **Financing-document extraction and comparison** | Extract parties, amounts, dates and terms from agreements; compare them with a financing register | Verified financing register and discrepancy list |
| **4** | **Interest calculation and repayment scenarios** | Apply explicitly supplied interest rates, day-count conventions and repayment terms; compare scenarios | Formula-based interest and repayment workbook |
| **5** | **Compliance and document-renewal tracker** | Convert an approved checklist into obligations, owners, due dates and evidence requirements | Compliance calendar and pending-document report |
| **6** | **Quarterly and annual financial-statement analysis** | Compare reporting periods, calculate agreed ratios and highlight changes for listed or unlisted companies | Financial comparison workbook and analytical summary |
| **7** | **Repeatable company research and news updates** | Use a company list, supplied reports, permitted Screener exports and approved news sources to prepare updates | Source-linked company briefs and research-update log |
| **8** | **Customised fundamental stock screener** | Translate selected financial conditions into editable formulas and explain each inclusion or exclusion | Configurable screener with pass/fail reasons |
| **9** | **Price-and-volume stock scanner** | Evaluate conditions such as price breakouts and unusual volume using historical observations | Scanner results with trigger explanations |
| **10** | **CMP watchlist and price-refresh workflow** | Map stock identifiers, import prices and display timestamps and data status | Price-monitoring workbook |
| **11** | **Single-page market-breadth dashboard** | Calculate advances/declines, participation indicators and customised measures for a defined universe | Market dashboard with data-coverage checks |
| **12** | **Trade-margin monitoring and exception reporting** | Analyse approved margin inputs, calculate headroom and shortfalls, and prepare exception alerts | Margin-monitoring dashboard and draft alerts |
